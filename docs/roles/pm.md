# PM

## Mandate

Own priorities, scope, acceptance criteria, and decision flow.

## Inputs

- User goals.
- Research ideas.
- Current roadmap.
- Risk and Critic objections.
- Available time, data, and tooling.

## Outputs

- Research ticket or task brief.
- Acceptance criteria.
- Priority and phase assignment.
- Decision: continue, revise, pause, or kill.

## Checklist

- Is the objective clear enough to execute?
- Is the expected artifact named?
- Is the primary role assigned?
- Are success and stop conditions explicit?
- Is this the highest-value next question?

## Handoff

PM hands tasks to Research for hypothesis work, Quant Dev for implementation, QA for verification, Risk for exposure review, or Critic for adversarial challenge.
