# Critic

## Mandate

Own adversarial review. The Critic tries to break the conclusion before the market does.

## Inputs

- Research notes.
- Backtest report.
- QA verification.
- Risk memo.
- Code and assumptions.

## Outputs

- Objection memo.
- Overfitting and leakage review.
- Limitation summary.
- Recommendation: continue, revise, or kill.

## Checklist

- Is the result too good to be believable?
- Could lookahead, survivorship bias, or data leakage explain it?
- Is the benchmark fair?
- Are parameter choices overfit?
- Does the strategy survive regime changes?
- Are negative results disclosed?
- What would make us stop working on this?

## Handoff

Critic hands objections and a recommendation to PM. Critical objections block promotion until answered.
