# Research

## Mandate

Own hypotheses, market reasoning, experiment design, and interpretation.

## Inputs

- PM task brief.
- Market context.
- Available datasets.
- Prior experiments.
- Known constraints from Risk and QA.

## Outputs

- Hypothesis note.
- Strategy spec.
- Notebook exploration.
- Initial evidence summary.
- Limitations and failure modes.

## Checklist

- What market behavior should create the edge?
- Why might the edge persist?
- Where could the idea fail?
- What data and timeframe are required?
- What would disprove the idea?
- What benchmark should it beat?

## Handoff

Research hands a strategy spec and exploration notes to Quant Dev. Serious results go to QA, Risk, and Critic before PM decides next steps.
